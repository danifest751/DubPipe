# Code of conduct

**English** · [Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ru.md)

## The short version

Be decent. Discuss the code, not the person.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion technical and specific. "This breaks on files without an audio track" is
  useful; "this is garbage" is not.
- Assume the other side is trying to help. Most disagreements here come from missing
  context, not bad intent.
- Accept that a change can be declined. The project has a stated shape and a stated
  boundary (private use, no distribution features); a "no" to a feature is not a "no" to you.
- Ask before doing large work. A short issue first saves everyone a wasted weekend.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning remarks about anyone — including about their
  language skills, experience level or nationality.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwanted personal attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Persistent off-topic disruption after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space of this project, and
to private contact that arises from them.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the Security
tab, or by contacting the maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close threads, or block accounts, and will say
what rule was broken when doing so. Repeated or severe violations lead to a permanent block.
